# Code of conduct

This project is for people learning and teaching AI. Everyone taking part is expected to:

- Be kind and patient, especially with beginners.
- Assume good faith, and disagree about ideas, not people.
- Give feedback that is specific and useful.
- Keep discussions on topic and free of harassment, discrimination and personal attacks.

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, close issues and pull requests, or block people who do not follow this.
